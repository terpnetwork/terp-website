Overview

Terp Network is a public proof-of-stake blockchain built with the Cosmos SDK. Anyone can run a node, verify the history, stake, vote, deploy CosmWasm contracts, or move tokens over IBC — without an account with us and without a bridge operator in the middle.

It uses two tokens: TERP to secure the chain and carry the vote, and THIOL, minted every block, to pay for transactions. Mainnet is morocco-1; the testnet is 120u-1. Source is public.

Consensus and staking

Validators propose blocks and vote on them in two rounds. A block is final once validators holding more than two-thirds of the staked TERP have signed it, so the chain stays safe as long as less than one-third of that stake misbehaves. A validator caught signing two different blocks at the same height is jailed and slashed.

Anyone holding TERP can delegate it to a validator and share in its rewards. Staked TERP is also voting weight in governance.

A history anyone can check

Each block records the hash of the block before it, together with the signatures of the validators that committed that block, who hold more than two-thirds of the stake. Changing any past block would change its hash and break every block and signature after it, so a rewritten history cannot pass as the real one.

You do not have to trust whoever serves you the data. A full node checks those signatures and replays every transaction itself; if its result differs from what the validators signed, it stops instead of following along. A light client, such as a wallet or another chain over IBC, checks the validator signatures on each new block header as it arrives.

Older history is kept in public archive snapshots. Start a node from one, compare its block hashes with any other node, and replay forward from there. Mainnet has produced blocks since 2023-04-10; the snapshot history lists which heights each archive covers, with every upgrade halt marked.

Governance

Changes to the chain are proposed and voted on openly. TERP stakers vote Yes, No, Abstain or No with veto on parameter changes, community-pool spending and software upgrades; a validator's vote counts for its delegators unless they vote themselves. A passed upgrade names a halt height, and node operators switch binaries there, often automatically with Cosmovisor.

CosmWasm smart contracts

Programs are written in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly and run by every validator. A contract can hold funds, call other contracts and send IBC messages. Any account can also create its own native token with tokenfactory; it behaves like any other coin in balances, transfers and IBC.

Smart accounts and authenticators

Any account can register authenticators: rules that decide which signatures and which messages the account accepts. A transaction names the authenticator it wants checked. The building blocks are:

  • SignatureVerification: a public key that may sign for the account, such as a second device key.
  • CosmwasmAuthenticatorV1: a CosmWasm contract judges the transaction, for example one that accepts signatures from an Ethereum key.
  • MessageFilter: limits which messages may be sent.
  • AllOf and AnyOf: combine others, so every member or any one of them must approve.

You can add and remove authenticators for your own account from the wallet panel under Connect.

IBC

The Inter-Blockchain Communication protocol moves tokens and messages between chains. Each side runs a light client of the other and checks its consensus proofs, so there is no bridge operator to trust. Tokens arriving from another chain appear as ibc/… denoms. With Interchain Accounts, an account or contract on one chain can control an account on another.

Genesis & distribution

Terp Network launched on 2023-04-10 from one public genesis file for chain morocco-1. Here is how it got there and where its supply started.

History

From an Ethereum token, through Chronic Chain, to Terp Network. Select an event for details and sources.

Chronic Token (CHT) on Ethereum

An ERC-20 token, CHT, was deployed on Ethereum on 2021-07-20 at 23:39:44 UTC (block 12,866,394). Uniswap liquidity was added eleven minutes later. Its supply was 420,000,000 CHT. The token had its own internal team.

The CHT exploit

On 2021-10-26 an address used the token contract’s _burn function on the CHT/WETH Uniswap pair. At 08:49:36 UTC (block 13,492,015) a contract it had deployed burned 55,782,062 CHT held by the pair, then sold 1,881.8 CHT into it for 167.94 WETH. The next day the token was publicly reported as hacked.

Sources: burn and swap transaction · first _burn call · “Chronic Token CHT hacked” report, 2021-10-27.

Chronic Chain

Chronic Chain was built by the Chronic Token team together with active members of its community. It was a Cosmos SDK chain with two tokens: CHT (420,000,000) and CGAS. Earlier ERC-20 CHT holders were offered a presale and a one-to-one CGAS distribution. Its mainnet, chain morocco-1, started on 2022-04-16 16:00 UTC and was relaunched from a new genesis on 2022-07-06 17:00 UTC.

Chronic Chain shut down by governance

Chronic Chain’s governance voted to shut the chain down. Its presale had used distribution methods that could not be verified, so the priority became transparent, retroactive integrity for every initial distribution and allocation, the standard Terp Network’s public genesis file is held to.

Source: chain-registry #757 (“sunset via governance”).

Terp Network launch

Terp Network went live on 2023-04-10 as chain morocco-1 (the chain id Chronic Chain had used). Both TERP and THIOL start at 420,000,000.

The genesis file came out of a public ceremony: the Terp testnet athena-1 was submitted to the chain registry in October 2022, validators submitted their genesis transactions between 4 and 7 April 2023, and the final genesis file was published on 2023-04-10. The network started from that file after the ceremony.

The file’s genesis_time field is set to 2022-04-10 12:00:00 UTC; the launch date of the live network is the ceremony date above.

Sources: chain-registry #890 · terpnetwork/genesis-prelaunch (gentxs, final genesis commit) · genesis file.

TerpNET Foundation

The TerpNET Foundation started as a DAO DAO organisation on Juno, created on 2022-10-25 16:46 UTC. Its first proposal, adding the Terp Network logo, passed on 2022-10-26. It has since moved to a DAO on Terp Network itself, created at block 20,972,857. The genesis plan lists 63000000UTERP & 63000000UTHIOL for the Foundation DAO, with 50% at 7 years vesting.
