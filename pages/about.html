About

Terp Network exists so anyone can verify, participate, and leave with everything — no permission, no custodian, no trusted bridge in the middle.

Verifiable before trusted

Releases ship checksums next to binaries, builds reproduce from source, and every claim on this site resolves to an endpoint you can query yourself.

Permissionless to join

Run a node, validate, build, or just query state. Nothing here requires an account with us — your keys are the only credential.

Sovereign by default

Self-custody, open Hermes relayers anyone can fund, governance decided by stake — not by an admin panel or a bridge multisig.

Interoperable, not wrapped

IBC light clients carry state between chains. No wrapped IOUs, no bridge operator to trust or to fail.
