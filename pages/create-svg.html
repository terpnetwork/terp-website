      Template rules
        Example templates

        Paste one into any assistant, then paste its answer into Template and Variables. Prompts include your current template and its open issues.

          • Cost: one transaction to the SVG minter. You pay gas in THIOL; there is no creation fee. The gas estimate for your template is under Size and gas.
          • Permanent: template, variables, seed, supply and price tiers are fixed once created. Pausing and the whitelist can change later.
          • Who can create: when the minter has an owner, only the owner can create collections.
